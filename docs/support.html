Support

Email musa@menubank.app and you will get a reply. Common questions are answered below.

Before anything else

PillClock only reminds you about medication you and your doctor have already agreed on. It never recommends a medication, a dose or a schedule, and it never tells you to start, stop, change or skip anything.

If you are wondering whether to take a medication, whether to stop one, or what a symptom means, ask a medical doctor or a pharmacist. Do not use this app, or any app, to make that decision. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

The alarm did not ring

  • Open Settings in PillClock and check the permission banner at the top of Today.
  • On iOS 26 and later PillClock uses the system alarm, which rings even in silent mode. Allow it when asked.
  • If you turned Vibration off in Settings, the reminder becomes a silent on-screen notification instead of a full-screen alarm. That is the trade-off: iOS cannot show a full-screen alarm without vibration.
  • Check that the medication's sound is not set to Silent if you expect sound.
  • Make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is not configured to suppress the app.

It rang again after I tapped "Taken"

This was a bug in an early build and is fixed. Update to the latest version. If it still happens, email us with the time and the medication name.

I cannot edit or delete a medication

Tap the medication row in the Medications tab to open its detail page — Edit is at the top right, Delete at the bottom. You can also tap ⋮ on any row in the list for Edit and Delete directly. Tapping a dose on the Today screen opens the same page.

I deleted a medication but a dose is still in my history

When you delete a medication, the "keep history" switch is on by default. That keeps your past record of doses already taken. Those entries live in the History tab. Turn the switch off while deleting if you want everything removed.

Subscription

  • The first week is free. If you do not cancel before it ends, the monthly subscription starts automatically and renews each month.
  • Cancel anytime: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling in the app alone does not stop billing.
  • New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases.
  • Billing problems, refunds and receipts are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backup and moving to a new phone

Settings → Backup creates a file you save wherever you like. You can protect it with a password. On the new phone, install PillClock and restore that file. The backup is not uploaded anywhere — if you lose the file, we cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

Showing data to my doctor

Settings → Export produces a PDF or CSV of your history and adherence rate. Share or print it however you like.

Languages

PillClock is available in Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Russian and Chinese, and follows your device language by default.

Still stuck?

Email musa@menubank.app. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Please do not send medical details — we do not need them and we do not want them.

PillClock is a reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Never start, stop, change or skip a medication based on this app. Those decisions belong to a qualified medical doctor who knows your history.
