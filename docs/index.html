Medication reminders that actually ring

PillClock is a straightforward medication reminder for iPhone. It opens full screen on your lock screen and rings, so a dose does not quietly slip past you.

What PillClock is — and is not

PillClock helps you keep track of medication you and your doctor have already decided on. It does nothing else.

  • It never recommends a medication, a dose, or a schedule.
  • It never suggests that you start, stop, change or skip anything.
  • It gives no diagnosis, no medical interpretation, no health advice of any kind.
  • It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional care.

Any decision about taking or stopping a medication must be made by a qualified medical doctor. If something feels wrong, contact your doctor or pharmacist — not an app. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What it does

  • A real alarm. Full screen on the lock screen, with sound if you want one.
  • One tap. Taken, Snooze (15 min) or Skip, straight from the lock screen.
  • Your schedule. Daily, once or twice a week, every 2 or 3 days, once a month.
  • Up to 6 doses a day, each with its own time.
  • Context. Empty or full stomach, a note, and a per-medication sound.
  • Supply tracking. It tells you before you run out.
  • History. Adherence over time, exportable as PDF or CSV to show your doctor.
  • Backup. You create the file, you decide where it goes.
  • Optional lock. Face ID, fingerprint or PIN.
  • Built to be readable. Large text, high contrast, screen reader support.

Nothing leaves your phone

PillClock has no internet permission at all. Not "we promise not to" — the app is built without the ability to make a network request, and that is verified automatically on every release build. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that could collect anything.

Your medication list, your times and your history live in a single database file on your device. Delete the app and all of it is gone.

Subscription

PillClock is free to download and requires a monthly subscription to set alarms. The first week is free. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store; PillClock never sees or stores your payment details. You can cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
