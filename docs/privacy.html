Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

PillClock collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere even if it wanted to. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. We — the developer — receive no data about you or your use of PillClock. We do not know that you installed it, we do not know what medications you take, and we have no way of finding out.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter stays in a single database file inside the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • Medication names, times, frequency and notes you type
  • Which doses you marked as taken, snoozed or skipped
  • Supply counts, if you use supply tracking
  • Your app settings (language, theme, sound, lock)

No other app can read this file. Automatic cloud backup of this file is switched off, so it is not copied to iCloud. Delete the app and all of it is removed from the device.

No internet permission

PillClock is built without network access. This is checked automatically before every release: the build fails if any network capability or third-party tracking library is present. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK of any kind.

Backups and exports

You can create a backup file or export your history as PDF or CSV. These files are created only when you ask for them, and PillClock hands them to you through the system share sheet. Where they go after that is entirely your choice. A backup can be protected with a password you set; without that password the file cannot be read. We never see any of these files.

Subscription and payments

PillClock requires a paid subscription to set alarms. The purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store. PillClock never sees, receives or stores your name, payment card, billing address or Apple Account details. The only thing stored on your device is whether a subscription is currently active.

Apple's own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Exemption codes

PillClock can be unlocked with a code given out by the developer. Entering a code changes a setting on your device only. Nothing is transmitted and no record of it reaches us.

Children

PillClock is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Because no data is collected, we have no way to notify you directly — and that is by design.

Contact

Questions about privacy: musa@menubank.app

PillClock is a reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Never start, stop, change or skip a medication based on this app. Those decisions belong to a qualified medical doctor who knows your history.
